I started the Polaris Project because of two of the latter: one I needed a personal project for the IB, and two I love airplanes. It may be silly, but during the summer prior to MYP5 I spent countless of hours brain storming what project I should create. The expense of having to design, fabricate, reiterate, wasn't ideal for me. I wanted something easy, something that wouldn't challenge me. And so I decided to choose the hardest one among my list, make an airplane!
This was my thought process where I used Canva when I was deciding and planning what project I should endeavour myself in, which finally lead me to the Polaris Project.
